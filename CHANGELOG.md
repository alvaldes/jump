# Changelog

Each `## <version>` section becomes that version's GitHub release notes
(`scripts/release.sh --upload`), and JUMP lists its top-level bullets under
"What's new" in Settings › General when that version is out. Keep each bullet
one short line about what a user gets. Write the next version's bullets under
`## Unreleased`, then rename the heading to the version before tagging.

## Unreleased

- Update notices: JUMP tells you in the menu bar menu and in Settings when a new version is out
- See what a new version brings, and choose how often to check, in Settings › General

## 1.1.0

- Excluded apps: JUMP's shortcuts stay off while the apps you choose are in front
- Choose the modifier for each click action: right-click, double-click or move the pointer

## 1.0.0

- Hints: label every clickable element of the focused window and type a label to click it
- Scroll mode: scroll any window with h j k l, d u and gg G
- Text search: find visible text and jump to it, with an optional OCR fallback
- Five hint themes, label sizes from 7 to 20 pt and Liquid Glass
- Three menu bar icons, an optional Dock icon and launch at login
